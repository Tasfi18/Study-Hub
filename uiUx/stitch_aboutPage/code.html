Our Journey

From a dorm room idea to a global community.

school

Founded in a Dorm Room

2023

It started with a simple observation: great notes were gathering dust after exams. We built the first version of StudyShare to help our roommates swap study guides.

group_add

First 10,000 Users

Early 2024

Word spread across campus and then to neighboring universities. Students loved the ease of access, and our community grew rapidly to over 10,000 active learners.

globe

Global Platform Launch

Late 2024

We officially opened our doors to the world, adding support for multiple languages and partnering with student organizations globally.
